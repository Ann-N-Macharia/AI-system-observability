Source: 1.evaluation/outputs/summary_evaluation_results.xlsx,
quality_gate_results.xlsx (n=15 clinical questions, balanced across USSD,
Mobile App, and Web Portal channels; 5 each).

  Metric            gpt-4o    gpt-4o-mini   Gate threshold
  BLEU-4            0.146     0.119         >= 0.130
  ROUGE-L           0.332     0.317         >= 0.300
  Token F1          0.455     0.442         >= 0.400
  Judge overall      4.78      4.89         >= 4.50 (1-5 scale)
  LLM-judge correctness/groundedness/helpfulness averaged 4.4-4.9/5 for

Clinical translation: judge scores indicate both models are safe on
correctness and groundedness for the sampled clinical scenarios. The
distinguishing factor is BLEU-4 — a stricter lexical-overlap check against
the clinical reference protocol — where gpt-4o-mini falls just short
(0.119 vs. 0.130 required), meaning its phrasing diverges from the
validated clinical wording more often than gpt-4o's, even when judged
"correct" in substance.

3. COST & EFFICIENCY ANALYSIS

Source: 3.cost/outputs/cost_summary.csv, cost_summary_prompt_type.csv,
cost_summary_lower_cost_model.csv, model_requirements.csv.

30-day cost projection at current 75/25 canary split: ~$1,436 total
(gpt-4o: ~$1,217 across the three features; gpt-4o-mini: ~$219).
Per-1M-token budget gates (model_requirements.csv): gpt-4o-mini clears
every cost gate (input and output); gpt-4o clears input-cost but FAILS
its own output-cost gate ($10.00 vs. a $7.00/1M ceiling) — an added
argument for shifting output-heavy features toward gpt-4o-mini where the
quality gate (Section 2) allows it.

Prompt configuration: chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting costs ~2x more per
request than direct prompting on both models (e.g. gpt-4o clinical
summaries: $0.00334/req CoT vs. $0.00170/req direct). CoT should be
reserved for features where the extra reasoning demonstrably improves the
judge/groundedness score enough to justify the multiplier.

Structural savings: routing 100% of traffic to gpt-4o-mini (the
lower-cost model) projects a 30-day spend of ~$146 vs. ~$1,436 under the
current split — a ~$1,290/month (≈90%) reduction, CONDITIONAL on
gpt-4o-mini clearing the BLEU-4 clinical-fidelity gate in Section 2. This
is the single largest cost lever available and should not be actioned
until that gate question is settled.

4. SYSTEMIC OPERATIONAL RISKS

Source: 2.drift/outputs/drift_trend_table.csv (3 simulated production
months vs. baseline).

  Month     ROUGE-L   Latency (ms)   Input tokens
  Month 1    0.417        798            223
  Month 2    0.382        959            287
  Month 3    0.292       1,317           376

Response quality (ROUGE-L) degraded ~30% from Month 1 to Month 3, while
latency rose ~65% and input token length rose ~69% over the same window —
a consistent, worsening trend rather than noise, suggesting either input
distribution shift (longer/more complex queries reaching the model) or
model-side degradation that compounds cost (Section 3) with quality risk.

5. ACTIONABLE ENGINEERING ROADMAP

1. Fix and re-run the drift alert pipeline before any further canary
   expansion. Financial: undetected drift risks silent cost growth via
   longer completions (token trend above). Safety: undetected ROUGE-L
   decay in a clinical-guidance product is a patient-safety exposure that
   the current pipeline cannot flag today.

2. Resolve the quality-gate discrepancy (Section 2) and, if gpt-4o-mini
   clears BLEU-4 on re-verification, route eligible features to
   gpt-4o-mini. Financial: ~$1,290/month (~90%) projected savings.
   Safety: only action if the corrected gate confirms mini meets the
   0.130 BLEU-4 floor — do not route on judge-score parity alone, since
   the memo's own data shows judge and lexical metrics disagree on this
   pair.

3. Default to direct prompting for clinical_summaries and epidemic_alerts,
   reserving chain-of-thought (CoT) for triage_advisor only. Financial:
   cost_summary_prompt_type.csv shows CoT running ~$163/month more than
   direct on those two features alone at gpt-4o-mini rates (and several
   hundred dollars more on gpt-4o) — a premium Phase 1 currently has no
   judge-score data to justify, since prompt config was never isolated as
   an evaluation variable. Safety: triage_advisor is the highest-risk
   feature and keeps the extra reasoning step; any future CoT spend on
   the other two features should require a demonstrated judge-score lift
   before being approved.
